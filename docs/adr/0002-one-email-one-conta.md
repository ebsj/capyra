# One email belongs to one Conta Capyra

An authenticated email identifies at most one **Operador**, and that Operador participates in exactly one **Conta Capyra**. Agency clients who approve content are members of the agency Conta, not guests with a second login.

Allowing one person on many Contas would be more honest for freelancers and brand-side marketers who later buy Capyra themselves. It also creates a session, invitation and leak surface we are not taking in v1. The escape hatch is another email, or waiting for multi-conta.
