# Native social identity is globally exclusive

A verified **Identidade nativa** may have at most one active **Conexão social** in all of Capyra. Two Contas cannot publish as the same Instagram (or any other network) at the same time. Handle is not the identity: reconnecting after a rename must not silently replace a different account.

The alternative — allowing the same profile on an agency Conta and a creator Conta — matches real talent/agency overlaps, but makes billing, revocation and "who is the Capyra app on Instagram?" ambiguous. Moving a profile to another Conta requires an explicit disconnect first.
