# Conta Capyra is the isolation boundary

The paying customer is the agency or creator, not each brand they operate. Isolation, members, billing and login all hang on **Conta Capyra**. **Marca** is a commercial identity inside that space — never a tenant, never a separate invoice, never the agency's contractual client as a domain entity.

The alternative was one tenant per brand (or per agency client). That would match how agencies talk, but it would explode login, billing (R$ 49,90 is per connected social account, not per brand) and the rule that one email belongs to one Conta. Switching agencies is then disconnect-and-reconnect, not "move the Marca tenant."
